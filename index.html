Team Overview

Who we are and what we're building.

F1 Rangers is a student engineering team participating in the STEM Racing Competition by Stemplify. Our objective is to design, manufacture, test, and race a high-performance CO₂-powered miniature racing car using engineering principles, aerodynamic optimisation, precision manufacturing, teamwork, and innovation.

Vision

To achieve engineering excellence through innovation, precision, and competitive spirit.

Mission

To develop a competitive racing car while demonstrating creativity, technical knowledge, collaboration, and professional responsibility.
